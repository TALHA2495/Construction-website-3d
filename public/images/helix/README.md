# Helix card artwork

Nine architectural crops (`helix-01.jpg` … `helix-09.jpg`) used by the scroll-driven 3D helix section.

- All nine are **centered-cover crops derived from the two supplied daylight references**
  (`assets/reference/hero-desktop.jpg` and `assets/reference/hero-mobile.jpg`).
- Crops 01–05 come from the landscape reference; 06–09 from the portrait reference.
- Output size 1024 px wide, ~2.09:1 (the helix card aspect ratio: 2.8 / 1.34), JPEG quality 85.
- These are concept images of a concept residence. They are not evidence of completed
  customer projects.
- The helix renders cards in reversed asset order (cards 09 → 01 along the travel
  direction), matching the canonical Web Fueled reference (`cardNumber = COUNT - i`).
  The closing grid reuses cards 01–06.
